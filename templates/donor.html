Blood Donation Eligibility Criteria

  • Age should be between 18 to 65 years.
  • Weight should be at least 50 kg.
  • Must be in good health and feel well on the day of donation.
  • Should not have donated blood in the last 3 months.
  • No major illnesses or infections in recent weeks.
🩸 Do's & Don'ts Before Donating Blood
✔️ Do's:
  • Get at least 6–8 hours of sleep the night before donating.
  • Eat a healthy, low-fat meal at least 2 hours before donation.
  • Drink plenty of water before and after donating.
  • Wear a shirt with short sleeves or sleeves that can be rolled up easily.
  • Inform staff if you are taking any medications or have recent illnesses.
  • Relax and stay calm before and during the donation process.
  • Bring a valid photo ID and any donor card you may have.
❌ Don'ts:
  • Don’t donate on an empty stomach or after heavy exercise.
  • Don’t consume alcohol 24 hours before donating.
  • Don’t smoke 1 hour before or after donating blood.
  • Don’t donate if you have flu, cold, or any infectious disease.
  • Don’t drive or operate machinery immediately after donating.
  • Don’t lift heavy items for at least 5 hours after donation.
  • Don’t hide any health-related information from the medical staff.
